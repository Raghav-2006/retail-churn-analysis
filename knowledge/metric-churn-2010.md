# Churn (2010 definition) — DEPRECATED

> Deprecated June 2011. Use `metric-churn` (90-day window).

Under the 2010 definition a customer churned when they had **no order in the 365 days** before
the reporting date. Churn rate = churned customers / customers who had ever ordered.

The one-year window reacted too slowly: a customer who stopped in January was not counted until
the following January.
