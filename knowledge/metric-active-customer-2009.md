# Active customer (2009 definition) — DEPRECATED

> Deprecated June 2011, replaced by `metric-active-customer` (90-day window).

A customer was counted as active as of a reporting date if they had ordered in the last
**180 days** (six months) before it. This overstated the active base by roughly a third, because it kept
customers who had already stopped buying.
