# Sales team FAQ

**What counts as a large order?** Anything over **£500**. Flag these to the account manager.

**What is our cancellation rate?** About one in five invoices gets cancelled, so we quote the
**share of invoices that are cancelled**: cancelled invoices / all invoices.

**Who is an active customer?** Anyone who has ordered in the **last year**.

**Where do I find a customer's revenue?** Sum their invoices in the sales report; ignore credit
notes (cancellations).
