# Out-of-scope questions

The warehouse has **no**:
- cost, margin or profit data; no discounts;
- salesperson, sales channel or marketing campaign; no web traffic or conversion data;
- customer type or segment field (wholesale vs retail, trade vs consumer);
- revenue targets, budgets or forecasts;
- customer demographics or addresses (age, gender, name, email, city);
- inventory or stock levels; product categories (only free-text descriptions);
- reviews or ratings; delivery or shipping dates.

Questions that need any of these must be declined: say which data is missing rather than using a
proxy.
