# Peak season

**Peak season** is **September, October and November** (months 9-11) of a calendar year: the
pre-Christmas wholesale buying period. December is not peak: orders drop after the first week and
stop around 23 December.

"Peak-season share" of a year's revenue = revenue in September-November / revenue in the whole
calendar year x 100.
